## Post 2 — Demo video (30s+, post after app is live)

**Script / shot list:**
1. (0-4s) Screen: Verdikt landing page. VO/caption: "Verdikt — get the crowd to judge your work, paid on-chain."
2. (4-10s) Connect wallet (Monad testnet). Caption: "Connect wallet."
3. (10-16s) Create task: upload 3 thumbnails, set 0.5 MON per vote, 60s window, confirm tx. Caption: "Escrow MON, post your thumbnails."
4. (16-24s) Switch wallet. Label view: tap the best thumbnail on 3 items, confirm votes. Caption: "The crowd votes. Tally is on-chain."
5. (24-30s) Window closes. Hit Claim, MON lands in wallet. Show the tx on testnet.monadexplorer.com. Caption: "Window closes, payout is automatic. No middleman."
6. End card: "Verdikt · live on Monad testnet · [URL]"

Record at phone aspect (9:16) for socials, keep a 16:9 too.

## Post 3 — Creative ad video concept (post separately)

**Concept: "The Thumbnail Court"** — 15-20s.
- Cold open: a creator stares at 3 near-identical thumbnails, paralyzed. Text: "Which one gets the click?"
- Cut: they drag the thumbnails into Verdikt. A crowd of wallet avatars swipes through them fast, tally bars fill live.
- Payout animation: coins stream from the escrow box to the voters.
- Tagline card: "Stop guessing. Put it to the crowd. **Verdikt.**"
- Sub-line: "On-chain. Trustless. Live on Monad."

Cheap to make with screen-capture + simple motion graphics (Canva / CapCut). No actors needed.
